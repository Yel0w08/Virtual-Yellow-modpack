V-Yellow

Virtual-Yellow is a vanilla-like modpack focused on performance and enhancing the overall gameplay experience. It includes built-in voice chat and optional shaders. You can join vanilla servers without any issues :D It also supports controllers.

This modpack goal is to have good performance with vanilla-like feature that make the game better, you can join vanilla servers with the modpack, you also have controler support. this modpack is desined to make the best experiences for the players, Contact me at PikminTea@proton.me or contact@yel0w.fr
